Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 31, 2026 | Application ID: com.mindscale.games

Summary: MindScale respects your privacy. We collect minimal, anonymized telemetry strictly through Firebase Analytics to improve game performance and user experience. No personal data, location telemetry, or identity tracking is stored or shared.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy outlines how MindScale ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our mobile application. MindScale is designed to be a safe, fast-paced mental arithmetic practice application.

2. Data Collection & Analytics

To ensure app quality, track stability, and understand gameplay engagement across difficulty tiers, MindScale uses Firebase Analytics (provided by Google Inc.).

Firebase Analytics collects anonymized, non-personally identifiable events such as:

  • Screen Navigation: Aggregated counts of visits to Home, Game, Results, Stats, and Settings screens.
  • Game Performance Metrics: Mode selections (Classic vs. Arcade), chosen difficulty levels, accuracy ratios, and streak counts.
  • Technical Diagnostics: App lifecycle events (app launch, crash diagnostics, device model, operating system version).

No Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, IP location tracking, or device contact details are ever collected.

3. Local Storage

All your personal game progress and app preferences are stored exclusively on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore and Room Database:

  • Sound Effects & Haptic Feedback preferences
  • Personal best score streaks and round accuracy history

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by wiping the app data from your device settings.

4. Children's Privacy

MindScale is suitable for users of all ages, including children. We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policies. We do not collect or request personal data from children under the age of 13.

5. Data Security

Because all gameplay history is stored locally on your device and network communication is strictly limited to Google's standard Firebase Analytics SDK over encrypted SSL/TLS channels, your data remains secure.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect feature updates or legal compliance changes. Any updates will be posted directly within this document.
